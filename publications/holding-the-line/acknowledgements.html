Acknowledgements

Phil Myint · OrdoAnimi · 28 June 2026

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to my mentors, Richard Scott and Chris Birrell, whose wisdom, guidance, and example have had a lasting influence on my career.

Chris helped sharpen my strategic thinking and reinforced the importance of clarity, disciplined judgement, and turning professional experience into something others can use.

Richard shaped my understanding of leadership through more than a decade of working together. He trusted me with meaningful responsibility, gave me room to develop my judgement, corrected me when necessary, and supported me without taking ownership of the work away from me.

Their influence has been different but complementary. The standards, judgement, and values they helped instil in me are present throughout this series, and I remain deeply grateful for their mentorship and friendship.
